\chapter{Project Defense and Project Expo}
\label{chap:project-defense-expo}

\noindent
This chapter presents the project evaluation and dissemination processes conducted through
two rounds of project defense and participation in the SKE Project Expo. This chapter
documents the feedback received from evaluators, the revisions made in response to that
feedback, and the outcomes of those revisions. In addition, it discusses the team's experience
in presenting the project to a broader audience, including poster design, audience interaction,
and reflections on how the Expo contributed to the overall development of the project.
